LESSON TITLE: WORKPLACE MOTIVATION AND ENERGY MANAGEMENT
CEFR LEVEL: B2 (UPPER-INTERMEDIATE)
DURATION: 50 MINUTES
FORMAT: TEXT-ONLY LISTENING LESSON BASED ON "THE SPARK" PODCAST

---

ACTIVITY 1: 10 KEY WORDS AND PHRASES
DURATION: 6 MINUTES
MATERIALS: CHAT BOX / SHARED SCREEN
INSTRUCTIONS:
1. ASK STUDENTS: "WHAT COMES TO MIND WHEN YOU THINK ABOUT STAYING MOTIVATED AT WORK?" ELICIT 3 IDEAS.
2. PRESENT THE 10 TARGET WORDS RELATED TO THE PODCAST.
3. PRONOUNCE EACH WORD CLEARLY. STUDENTS REPEAT IN CHORUS AND INDIVIDUALLY.
4. QUICK CHECK: STUDENTS HOLD UP NUMBERS (1-10) MATCHING THE DEFINITION YOU READ.

WORD LIST:
1. autonomy (noun) -- freedom within a framework to make decisions
2. mastery (noun) -- continuous improvement and skill development
3. purpose (noun) -- connection to a human story and real impact
4. burnout (noun) -- physical and mental exhaustion from chronic overwork
5. reframing (noun) -- looking at a routine task from a new perspective
6. momentum (noun) -- forward movement created by small completions
7. agency (noun) -- ability to act and make choices for oneself
8. recovery (noun) -- rest and time to restore energy and judgment
9. engagement (noun) -- emotional commitment and connection to one's work
10. routine (noun) -- regular, repetitive tasks performed daily

>>> TEACHER NOTES: Ensure students understand that autonomy is not total freedom without structure, but freedom within a clear framework.

3. PAIRS PRACTICE READING THE DIALOGUE ALOUD WITH NATURAL INTONATION.

DIALOGUE LINES (SCRAMBLED ORDER):
A | Alex | "True freedom requires autonomy within a clear framework."

DURATION: 7 MINUTES

2. STUDENTS SEQUENCE THE LINES IN PAIRS.

DIALOGUE LINES (SCRAMBLED ORDER):
A | Manager | "Our team engagement has dropped because employees feel micromanaged."

ACTIVITY 6: ROLE PLAY
DURATION: 12 MINUTES
MATERIALS: ROLE PLAY PROMPTS
INSTRUCTIONS:
1. DIVIDE STUDENTS INTO PAIRS (MANAGER AND EMPLOYEE).
2. ASSIGN THE ROLE PLAY SCENARIO: A 1-ON-1 MEETING TO DISCUSS REGAINING MOTIVATION AND PREVENTING BURNOUT.
3. STUDENTS USE AT LEAST 5 OF THE TARGET VOCABULARY WORDS IN THEIR CONVERSATION.
4. OBSERVE PAIRS AND PROVIDE POSITIVE FEEDBACK ON FLUENCY AND VOCABULARY USE.

ROLE PLAY CARD A (MANAGER):
- YOU NOTICE YOUR EMPLOYEE IS SHOWING SIGNS OF BURNOUT.
- DISCUSS HOW TO INCREASE THEIR AUTONOMY AND PURPOSE.
- AGREE ON SMALL COMPLETIONS TO BUILD MOMENTUM.

ROLE PLAY CARD B (EMPLOYEE):
- YOU FEEL OVERWHELMED BY A REPETITIVE ROUTINE AND LACK OF RECOGNITION.
- ASK FOR CLEARER FRAMEWORKS AND DEDICATED RECOVERY TIME.
- DISCUSS WAYS TO FIND MASTERY IN YOUR CURRENT PROJECTS.
