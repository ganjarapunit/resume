Bao and Linh
2-Month English Plan

Everyday English for 2 people.

Speak. Listen. Together.

How to use:

✓ Do 1 or 2 lessons each week.

✓ One lesson = 60 minutes.

✓ Each lesson = 1. Learn 2. Say 3. Do together.

0 / 12 lessons done. Your ticks are saved on this device.

Week 1

Lesson 1: Hello!

1. Learn
  • Hello / Hi / Bye
  • I am Bao / I am Linh
  • husband / wife
2. Say - say 3 times
  • Hello! I am Bao.
  • Hi! I am Linh.
  • Nice to meet you!
3. Do together
  • Shake hands. Say hello and bye to each other.

Lesson 2: Where are you from?

1. Learn
  • from / live / in
  • Vietnam / city / home
2. Say - say 3 times
  • Where are you from?
  • I am from Vietnam.
  • We live in...
3. Do together
  • Ask and answer. Point to your city on your phone.

Week 2

Lesson 3: Numbers and Time

1. Learn
  • 1, 2, 3 ... 20
  • day / time / today
  • Monday ... Sunday
2. Say - say 3 times
  • What time is it?
  • It is 7 o'clock.
  • Today is Monday.
3. Do together
  • Look at your phone. Ask the time 5 times.

Lesson 4: Food at Home

1. Learn
  • rice / water / coffee
  • I want / I like
2. Say - say 3 times
  • I want rice.
  • I like coffee.
  • Do you want water?
3. Do together
  • Go to the kitchen. Point and say: I want...

Week 3

Lesson 5: At the Cafe

1. Learn
  • please / thank you
  • menu / bill
2. Say - say 3 times
  • One coffee, please.
  • Thank you!
  • The bill, please.
3. Do together
  • One is waiter. One is customer. Change!

Lesson 6: At the Market

1. Learn
  • how much / money
  • cheap / too much
2. Say - say 3 times
  • How much is this?
  • It is 20,000 dong.
  • Too much. Thank you!
3. Do together
  • Put 5 things on the table. Buy and sell.

Week 4

Lesson 7: Our Home

1. Learn
  • bed / kitchen / bathroom
  • where / here / there
2. Say - say 3 times
  • Where is the bed?
  • It is here.
  • It is there.
3. Do together
  • Walk in your home. Ask: Where is...?

Lesson 8: In the Street

1. Learn
  • left / right / straight
  • where / near / far
2. Say - say 3 times
  • Where is the market?
  • Go straight.
  • Turn left.
3. Do together
  • Draw a map. Tell your husband / wife: go!

Week 5

Lesson 9: I Feel Sick

1. Learn
  • sick / hot / pain
  • doctor / medicine
2. Say - say 3 times
  • I feel sick.
  • I need a doctor.
  • Where is the hospital?
3. Do together
  • One is sick. One is doctor. Help me!

Lesson 10: Phone Call

1. Learn
  • hello? / sorry / again
  • slowly / OK
2. Say - say 3 times
  • Hello? This is Bao.
  • Please speak slowly.
  • Sorry, say again?
3. Do together
  • Call each other in 2 rooms. Talk 2 minutes.

Week 6 - 7

Lesson 11: Free Time

1. Learn
  • like / love / weekend
  • walk / music / movie
2. Say - say 3 times
  • I like music.
  • Do you like movies?
  • Yes, I do. / No, I don't.
3. Do together
  • Plan your weekend in English. Saturday? Sunday?

Week 8

Lesson 12: We Speak English!

1. Learn
  • All words from Lesson 1-11
2. Say - say 3 times
  • Hello! I can speak English!
  • Thank you, my love!
  • See you again!
3. Do together
  • Cook and eat together. Speak only English 30 minutes!
