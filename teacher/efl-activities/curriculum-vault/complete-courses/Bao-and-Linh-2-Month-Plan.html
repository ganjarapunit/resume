Week 1: Meet people

Lesson 1A: Hello!

Day 1: Learn
1. Words
  • hello, evening, name
  • husband, wife, friend
  • meet, welcome
2. Sounds
  • H, h: hello
  • W, w: wife
3. Say - say 3 times
  • My name is Bao.
  • This is my wife, Linh.

Lesson 1B: Use it

Day 2: Use
1. Say again
  • Hello! My name is Bao.
  • Hi! My name is Linh. Nice to meet you!
2. Do together
  • Shake hands. Greet each other. Change roles.
  • Go out: greet one neighbour in English.
