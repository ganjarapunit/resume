Activity 8 of 15 . Grammar

Write here or there

Read. Write here or there. Then Check.

1. You are in the bedroom. Where is the bed? It is.
2. You are in the bedroom. Where is the kitchen? It is.
3. You are in the bathroom. Where is the bathroom? It is.
4. You are in the kitchen. Where is the door? It is.
Check

1 here. You are in the bedroom. 2 there. The kitchen is not here. 3 here. You are in the bathroom. 4 here or there. Both are possible.

Activity 12 of 15 . Info gap

Read your card only

Read your card. Hide it. Talk.

Bao
You are the host. Your home: bed in the bedroom. The kitchen is on your left. The bathroom is there. You know where everything is. Answer: It is here. Or: It is there.
Linh
You are the guest. You do not know this home. Ask: Where is the bed? Where is the kitchen? Where is the bathroom? Find all three rooms.
Check

Guest asks: Where is the bed? Host: It is here. Guest: Where is the kitchen? Host: It is there. Guest: Where is the bathroom? Host: It is there. On your left. Then tap Change roles and talk again.
