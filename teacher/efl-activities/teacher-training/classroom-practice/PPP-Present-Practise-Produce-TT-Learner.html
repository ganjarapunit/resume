Start here

Good lessons have 3 steps.

Today you learn PPP. You will use it in Teaching Practice 1.

  • 1. Show new language in context
  • 2. Help learners practise safely
  • 3. Use language to communicate

Why PPP

New language is hard.

First we show. Then we help. Then learners speak free. No overload.

ShowHelpUse

The 3 parts

Present. Practise. Produce.

  • Present: show meaning, form, sound. 10 mins.
  • Practise: small safe tasks. 12 mins.
  • Produce: real talk with a goal. 15 mins.

Golden rules

5 rules for TP1.

  • 1. Short show. Long use.
  • 2. Check, do not ask Do you understand?
  • 3. Drill sound every time.
  • 4. Give a clear goal for talk.
  • 5. Correct late in free talk.

Present: pick your letter

Show A, B, C, or D.

Pick your TP letter first. Then use only its Show box.

A: going toShow the mum photo. Say: She is going to visit her mum. Ask: Did she do it already? No. Is it a plan? Yes.
B: past simpleShow a film photo. Say: I watched a film last night. Ask: Did I watch it yesterday? Yes. Is it now? No.
C: canShow a swim photo. Say: I can swim. I cannot fly. Ask: Can I swim? Yes. Can I fly? No.
D: comparativesShow two towns. Say: My town is bigger than your town. Ask: Which town is big? My town. Are they the same? No.

Practise: same letter

Stay with your letter.

Safe gaps. The learner says the full sentence. You correct at once.

A: going to gapsShe ___ visit her mum. Answer: is going to. Say it. Then type it.
B: -ed gapsI watch___ a film last night. Answer: watched. Say it. Then read it aloud.
C: can gapsI ___ swim. She ___ fly. Answers: can, cannot. Say each full sentence.
D: -er gapsMy town is ___ than yours. Answer: bigger. Say it. Then type it.

Produce: same letter

Talk with your letter.

Real talk with a goal. Your learner must use the target form.

A: weekend plansAgree on 2 shared plans together. Say each plan with going to.
B: last weekendTell 4 actions from last weekend. Find 2 actions you both did. Use watched, played, cooked.
C: abilities cardMake a card with 4 true sentences with can. Read 2 aloud. Example: I can cook.
D: best townCompare 2 towns with bigger, smaller, cleaner. Choose the best place to visit and say why.

Your 1-1 TP: pick one path

Choose A, B, C, or D.

One letter from start to end. Use its boxes on Slides 11, 12, and 13.

A: Online, A2Teach going to for weekend plans. Outcome: agree on 2 shared plans together.
B: Classroom, A2Teach past simple for last weekend. Example: I watched a film. Outcome: tell 4 actions and find 2 you both did.
C: Classroom, A1Teach can and cannot for abilities. Example: I can swim. Outcome: make a card with 4 true sentences and read 2 aloud.
D: Online, A2Teach comparatives for towns. Example: My town is bigger than your town. Outcome: compare 2 towns and choose the best place to visit.

Plan it in 20 minutes

Plan PPP plus feedback.

Stay with one letter A to D through Slides 11, 12, and 13. Plan 30 minutes.

1. AimWrite one aim with your letter. Example B: my learner will talk about last weekend with past simple.
2. StagesShow your letter box. Drill its sound. Practise its gaps. Produce its talk.
3. Delayed feedbackListen in produce. Write 4 sentences you hear. Fix them together. Drill the correct forms.
4. CheckAsk your learner to say the real plans again with the correct form.
