At the chemist

A short chemist dialogue. Sore throat, cough, lozenges, 7.49 pounds.

Tap Next. Read first. Then listen. Answer as you go.

How to use

  1. Read Activities 1 to 3 first.
  2. On Activities 4 to 9, use the Listen bar under the slide. Play twice.
  3. Choose or type. Tap Check. Then open Answer key.
  4. Speak aloud for Activities 13 to 15.

Our plan

Before: predict, words. While: gist, detail. After: use, speak.

One task at a time. Short steps. No rush.

Activity 1, Warmer

How are you today?

Choose one, then say it aloud: great, OK, tired, sick.

Finish: Today I feel ___ because ___.

Answer key (try first)

Any true sentence. Example: Today I feel OK because I slept well.

Activity 2, Predict

What will we hear?

A chemist is a shop for medicine in the UK. Tick what you expect.

Choose the two true ideas. Two options are correct. Select one, then Check, then try the other.

Answer key (try first)

Symptoms, medicine choice and price. Not car repair or football.

Activity 3, Words

Match the words

1 sore throat, 2 cough, 3 syrup, 4 lozenges, 5 prescription, 6 allergic

Answer key (try first)

1 pain in the throat. 2 fast air from the throat. 3 a, liquid medicine. 4 small sweets for the throat. 5 paper from a doctor. 6 bad reaction to medicine.

Activity 4, Gist

Why is he there?

Answer key (try first)

His wife sent him. He needs medicine for his throat.

Activity 5, Symptoms

What are his problems? Choose 2.

Tip: he says no to headache. Select one true answer and Check, then find the second.

Answer key (try first)

Sore throat and cough. No headache. No toothache.

Activity 6, Choice

Syrup or lozenges?

Answer key (try first)

Both are good. Syrup costs more. He takes the lozenges.

Activity 7, Dose

How much and when?

Answer key (try first)

1 one. 2 four to six. 3 before. Line: Just one every four to six hours, before meal times.

Activity 8, True or false

True or false?

Answer key (try first)

1 F, no allergy. 2 F, need a prescription. 3 T. 4 T.

Activity 9, Language

What does the pharmacist say?

Three are true. Check one at a time.

Answer key (try first)

Do you have a headache too? Are you allergic to any medicine? Anything else? Not the car.

Activity 10, Use the words

Choose the verb

1 ___ lozenges.

2 ___ a prescription.

Answer key (try first)

1 take lozenges. 2 need a prescription. 3 see a doctor.

Activity 11, Polite voice

Say it politely

Say aloud: Can I get some antibiotics too? How often should I take it?

Which is soft?

Answer key (try first)

Can I get is soft. Stress get, often, take. Polite rise at end.

Activity 12, Advice

Finish with should

Answer key (try first)

1 should. 2 should not. Should is advice, not a rule.

Activity 13, Speak

You at the chemist

Say 6 lines aloud: hello, problem, ask for medicine, ask dose, ask price, thank.

Start: Hello. I need something for ___.

Answer key, model (speak first)

Hello. I need something for a sore throat. I cannot stop coughing. Which is better? How often should I take it? How much is it? Thank you.

Activity 14, Doctor

Tell the doctor

Say 3 sentences aloud. Use: I have... for 3 days. I took... It hurts when...

Answer key, model (speak first)

I have a cough for 3 days. I took lozenges every 5 hours. It hurts when I swallow.

Activity 15, Takeaway

What can you say now?

Answer key (try first)

Any clear sentence. Example: Next time I will say: I need something for a cold. Well done.
