Think of your real kitchen, street, morning. Real life makes each wh-clause stick.

Pattern - 15 seconds

Main idea + wh + subject + verb - no flip inside. Say where he is not where is he.

where
place - where fruit is fresh
what
thing - what you want
who
person - who can help
how
way - how you make coffee
which
choice - which bus is faster

Your day

Your true morning, shop, home. One sentence each.

1. Which morning do you like - quiet or busy?

2. What do you eat or drink first?

3. Who helps you at home?

4. Where do you shop or relax?

5. How do you unwind?

Say it aloud

Say two answers out loud.

I like a quiet morning where it is calm. I have toast and what is quick. My sister helps, I ask who is free. I shop where fruit is fresh. I unwind how I like, with music.

noticeDONE

6. I will meet you ___ we met yesterday.

What each means

meaningDONE

Place, thing, person, way, choice.

WHERE in where fruit is fresh
WHAT in what you want
WHO in who can help
HOW in how you make coffee
WHICH in which bus is faster

Say it

Say: WHERE means place.

WHERE place, WHAT thing, WHO person, HOW way, WHICH choice. WHICH needs options you can see.

Choose the wh

gap fillDONE

Type where what who how which.

2. Tell me you need from shop.

what you need, not what do you need. Statement order inside.
5

Which or what

choice vs openDONE

WHICH for seen options. WHAT for open.

1. Tell me you want, anything.
2. Tell me sandwich, tuna or chicken?
3. I cannot decide to cook.
4. Choose route, fast or scenic?
5. Ask her she thinks.
6. Do you know bus, 12 or 24?

See or or choose - often WHICH.

Say it

Say 1 and 2 back to back.

WHAT open, WHICH limited. Sentence 3 both ok, context decides.

Fix it

word orderDONE

No flip inside. wh + subject + verb.

Type the wh part only.

1. I know where is the bakery.
2. Tell me what do you want.
3. Show me how do you make coffee.
4. I will go where do you go.
5. Do you know who is he?

Say it

Say: I know where the best bakery is.

1 where the best bakery is
2 what you want
3 how you make
4 where you go
5 who he is
