Routines vs Right Now

Present Simple & Continuous DONE

Goal: Use the Present Simple for habits and the Present Continuous for what is happening now.

Complete the verb in each sentence.

I usually work at 9 a.m.
Right now I a team meeting.
She emails every morning.
Today she a new colleague.

Speak it aloud

Tell your screen: one thing you do every day at work, and one thing you are doing today.

I check my email at 8 a.m. Today I am writing a report for my boss.

The Meeting That Went Wrong

Past Simple & Continuous DONE

Goal: Use the Past Simple for finished actions and the Past Continuous for an action in progress.

Complete the verb in each sentence.

Yesterday I the bus.
At 8 a.m. I at the wrong stop.
While I my boss, the train arrived.
We the problem last night.

Speak it aloud

Tell a short work story. Use a Past Continuous sentence for the background, like: "I was sending an email when the system crashed."

I was presenting online when my internet dropped. I fixed it after five minutes.

How Long Have You Been

Present Perfect Continuous DONE

Goal: Use the Present Perfect Continuous for an action from the past until now.

Complete the verb in each sentence.

She Spanish since March.
They for the client all morning.

Before I Started

Past Perfect DONE

Goal: Use the Past Perfect for the earlier of two past events.

Complete the verb in each sentence.

I the report before the meeting began.
She the email when I called.
They before we arrived.

Speak it aloud

Say one thing you had done before you started your current job (study, travel, or another role).

I had finished university before I started working here.
6

Plans vs Decisions

Going to & Will DONE

Goal: Use "going to" for plans and "will" for a sudden decision.

Complete the verb in each sentence.

I for the manager role. (plan)
"The printer is broken!" "OK, I you." (now)
We the new site next month. (plan)

Speak it aloud

Say one work plan you have (going to) and one thing you will do if a problem happens today.

I am going to email the client this afternoon. If the call drops, I will call them back.
7

This Time Next Year

Future Continuous & Perfect DONE

Goal: Use Future Continuous for action in progress later, and Future Perfect for finished by a time.

Complete the verb in each sentence.

This time tomorrow I to Berlin.
By December I the training.
This time next week she the workshop.

Speak it aloud

Predict: "This time next year, I will be (verb ing)..." and "By 2027 I will have (past participle)..."

This time next year I will be managing a team. By 2027 I will have learned French.

A Day in the Life

Mixed Tenses DONE

Goal: Mix past, present, and future tenses in one work story.

Complete the verb in each sentence.

I always coffee at 8. (habit)
Yesterday at 9 I the stand up. (past)
Right now I a proposal. (now)
I two emails. (result now)
At 5 I the budget. (future)

Speak it aloud

Build a five sentence "day in the life" using past, present, and future tenses.

I drink coffee at 8. Yesterday I joined the meeting. Now I am writing a proposal. I have sent two emails. At 5 I will review the budget.

Tense Switch

Transform Sentences DONE

Goal: Change a sentence from one tense to another. Type the new sentence.

Change each sentence to the tense named.

"I send the report." -> PAST:
"She is training Sam." -> PRESENT PERFECT:
"We will launch in May." -> PAST:
"They finished the task." -> PRESENT PERFECT:
"He is calling the client." -> PAST:

Speak it aloud

Say each changed sentence out loud before you type it. This trains your ear and mouth together.

I sent the report. / She has trained Sam. / We launched in May. / They have finished the task. / He was calling the client.

Tense Detective

Goal: Name the tense in real messages, then write your own in that tense.

Choose the tense of each workplace message.

1. "I am writing to confirm the meeting."
2. "I have completed the slides."

Reply to message 2 with your own sentence in the same tense (Present Perfect).

I have finished the report. / I have spoken to the client this morning.
