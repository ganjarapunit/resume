Write the reflection Complete

5 min

Aim for 150 to 180 words.

Words: 0. Target: 150 to 180.
Original 176-word practice model, available after your attempt

Across 25 days, I moved from writing separate paragraphs to connecting one clear point to a reader. I can now state a position, support it with relevant evidence, and add a fair limitation. My writing is more direct because I stopped using general praise and added details from my own work.

The error list taught me that revision means changing one part for a reason, not rewriting everything. My 1 to 5 scores also helped me see progress. I still need to check organisation and verb forms more quickly.

My process engineering context gives me useful evidence, but I learned not to use every number. The 30-day Pertamina RU IV audit can support a safety argument, while the K3 certificate can show how learning changed my thinking. Other numbers belong only when they prove the point.

Next, I will build a small writing portfolio. I want one report, proposal, CV, SOP, review, and blog, each with its brief, final text, score, and revision. This will show my voice improving across genres, not only in one essay.

This is original practice material, not published learner work.

Words: 0. Target: 100 to 130.
Original 120-word practice model, available after your attempt

Dear future me,

You have finished 25 writing days. Keep the error list, your 1 to 5 scores, and one final text from each genre. Your next goal is to organise the portfolio so it shows progress in clear, specific writing.

Your main obstacle may be finding time to revise and save every version. To solve it, reserve 20 minutes each week. First, choose six final texts. Then add each brief, score, and one revision note. Remove repeated files, but keep the evidence of improvement.

When you feel unsure, do not start with a blank page. Check the reader, purpose, one main point, and the action you want. Your voice is already clear. Keep it, and keep revising honestly.

Your name
