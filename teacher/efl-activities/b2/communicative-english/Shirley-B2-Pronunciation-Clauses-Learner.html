Answer key: how to use this deck

There is no score here. Try first, speak aloud, then open the key. Good work sounds calm, chunked, with key words loud and small words quick.

START

Goals and key

By the end you can:

  1. Pause after front clauses and around extra who info.
  2. Stress key work words, soften small words.
  3. Rise to continue, fall to finish.
Example: If we FINish TESTing by THURSday, ↗ | we can LAUNCH on MONday ↘
Answer key

Say the example in two chunks. Small rise after Thursday, clear fall after Monday. Loud on FIN, TEST, THURS, LAUNCH, MON.

ACTIVITY 3 OF 15 . RULE

Discover the rule

A. If we finish testing by Thursday, | we can launch.
B. Our manager, | who joined in March, | approved.
C. What I need | is a short summary.

Rules: 1 Extra who info, pause around it. 2 Front condition, pause after it. 3 What subject, pause before is.

Answer key

A-2, B-1, C-3. C is: What I need | is a short summary. Front clause pauses and rises. Extra who pauses like brackets. What subject pauses before the verb, then falls.

ACTIVITY 4 OF 15 . LISTEN

Same or different

Your teacher says two versions, or press play. Are they the same.

Model voice: hear flat versus chunked, and how pauses change meaning.

Pair 1: flat no pause, or chunked with pause. Which is clearer.

Answer key

Pair 1: Different. Chunked is clearer. Pair 2: Different. Meaning changes. The colleagues who finished early can leave (only some) vs The colleagues, who finished early, can leave (all did). Pair 3: Different. Natural stress wins. What I NEED is a SUMmary, not WHAT I need.

ACTIVITY 6 OF 15 . TUNE

Rise or fall

Listen. Does the voice go up to continue, or down to finish.

Model voice: rise holds your turn, fall closes it.

Answer key

1 rise ↗, 2 fall ↘, 3 rise ↗, 4 fall ↘. Rise holds your turn. Fall closes the thought. Hand up for rise, hand down for fall.

ACTIVITY 7 OF 15 . PAUSE

Chunk and read

Add |, then read aloud, slow then natural.

Model voice: copy these chunks and pauses.

  1. Although the budget was tight we hired two designers.
  2. The colleague who handles invoices is on leave.
  3. Can you confirm whether the meeting is still at ten.
Answer key

1. Although the budget was tight, | we hired two designers.
2. Defining, no pause needed. As extra news: The colleague, | who handles invoices, | is on leave.
3. Can you confirm | whether the meeting is still at ten.

ACTIVITY 8 OF 15 . WEAK FORMS

Loud on CAPS. Quick and soft on small words.

  2. The server WAS down. Say WAS as wuhz.
