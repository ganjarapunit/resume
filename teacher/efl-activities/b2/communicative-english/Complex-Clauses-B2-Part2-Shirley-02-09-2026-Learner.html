Smooth or clunky?

pick natural B2DONE

Choose the smoother B2 version.

A Although it was raining for hours before we went. B Although it had been raining for hours, we went.
A The house that Jack built it is famous. B The house Jack built is famous.
A I do not know what has she decided. B I do not know what she has decided.
A My brother who lives abroad has just arrived. (one brother) B My brother, who lives abroad, has just arrived.
A When I will have finished, I will call you. B When I have finished, I will call you.

Say it

Read each B smooth version once.

B in each is smoother for B2.
