Three clause types when travelling

B2 | Travel English | Use Back and Next to move. 15 activities plus cover and finish.

A = adverbial: even though, unless, in case B = attributive: who, whose, which, where C = noun clause: wonder, explain, decide

Even though we were exhausted, we walked to the old town. The itinerary, which our agent emailed yesterday, looks packed. I wonder whether the night bus still runs. Three links, one journey.

How this deck works. Select to open.

One slide at a time. Answer on the slide, press Check, open the key, then press Next. Say each answer aloud once.

Activity 1. Trips so far

Speak. 1 or 2 sentences each.

1. What was your best trip so far, and why?
2. Who do you usually travel with?
3. Where would you love to go next?

My best trip was Hoi An because the old town glows at night. I usually travel with my sister, who plans everything. I would love to visit Japan, where the trains run to the second.

Answer key. Select to open.

No fixed answers. Any true reply is good. Aim for one because, who, or where line.

Activity 2. Which sounds better on a trip?

Choose a or b.

1. a) Flight late. Wait here. b) As soon as the gate opens, please go straight there.

2. a) I want the hotel. It has a pool. b) I want a hotel that has a pool and is near the metro.

3. a) Where is passport control? Tell me. b) Could you tell me where passport control is?

Answer key. Select to open.
  1. b, as soon as gives exact timing.
  2. b, that picks the right hotel.
  3. b, polished noun clause.

Activity 3. Sort: A, B, or C?

A = when, why, on what condition. B = describes a noun. C = after know, wonder, remember.

1. We took a taxi because the metro was closed.

2. The guide who met us at arrivals spoke three languages.

3. I had no idea that the museum shuts on Mondays.

4. Unless the fog lifts, our flight cannot take off.

5. The guesthouse where we stayed had no lift.

6. I wonder whether the night bus still runs.

7. Even though we were exhausted, we walked to the old town.

8. The itinerary, which our agent emailed yesterday, looks packed.

9. Can you remember what time boarding starts?

Answer key. Select to open.

A: 1, 4, 7. B: 2, 5, 8. C: 3, 6, 9. Note the commas in 8: extra detail.

Activity 4. Adverbial: choose the right ending A

1. Even though the queue was huge, ...

2. Take a screenshot of the booking in case ...

3. Unless you check in online, ...

4. The night train was cheap, whereas ...

5. Call the hotel as soon as ...

Answer key. Select to open.

1 staff stayed calm and friendly. 2 your phone loses signal. 3 you may wait an hour at the desk. 4 the high speed one cost twice as much. 5 you land so they hold the room.

Activity 5. Adverbial: your travel lines A

Say, then type. Short lines are best.

1. I always pack light when ...
2. I would cancel a day trip if ...
3. I once got lost because ...
4. Even though I was nervous, I ...

I always pack light when I fly with budget airlines. I would cancel a day trip if the storm warning stays red. I once got lost because my map would not load. Even though I was nervous, I asked a stranger and found the hostel.

Answer key. Select to open.

Accept true lines. Check each linker plus a subject and verb after it.

Activity 6. who, whose, which, where, when B

Type one word. Whose shows owning. Commas mark extra detail.

Answer key. Select to open.
  1. whose
  2. when
  3. which or that
  4. who
  5. which or that
  6. where

Activity 7. Join two lines into one B

Type the key words. Example: whose flight.

1. We met a couple. Their flight had been cancelled twice.

2. I lost the voucher. You sent it last Friday.

3. She recommended a beach town. Few tourists go there.

4. Tom is the friend. I always travel with him.

Answer key. Select to open.
  1. We met a couple whose flight had been cancelled twice.
  2. I lost the voucher that you sent last Friday.
  3. She recommended a beach town where few tourists go.
  4. Tom is the friend who I always travel with.

Activity 8. Polished travel questions C

Type the end part. Use statement order. No flip.

Answer key. Select to open.
  1. where I can store my bag
  2. what time the shuttle leaves
  3. whether (or if) this seat is taken
  4. when the platform will be announced
  5. why our gate was changed

Activity 9. doubt, explain, decide C

1. I doubt ___ the last ferry still runs.

2. She explained ___ we had to rebook.

3. I cannot decide ___ to take the night bus or wait.

4. He claims ___ the view from that hostel is the best in town.

5. Nobody told us ___ the taxi would cost.

Answer key. Select to open.

1 whether. 2 why. 3 whether. 4 that. 5 how much.

Activity 11. Fix the chat: choose the right line

1. Even though it was crowded, but the staff were kind.

2. The woman who's bag was lost stayed calm.

3. Could you tell me where is the taxi rank?

4. The hotel where we stayed there had a rooftop pool.

5. He said me that the tour was full.

6. If the fog will not lift, we miss our connection.

Answer key. Select to open.

All first options are right. 1: no but with even though. 2: whose for owning. 3: statement order. 4: drop extra there. 5: told me, not said me. 6: if plus present, will in the main clause.

Activity 12. Travel desk: choose the natural reply

1. Guest: Our room is noisy.

2. Traveller: Where is my bus?

3. You need help with a map.

4. Plan for the ferry.

Answer key. Select to open.

All first options. They are fuller, politer, and use the clause correctly.

Activity 13. Write: six true travel lines

Write about your real trips. Then say each line aloud.

1. The trip that changed me most was ...
2. I feel most relaxed when ...
3. A place I know, where ..., keeps calling me back.
4. I have realised that good travel means ...
5. Unless I ..., I always ...
6. Ask one real travel question with Could you tell me ...?

1. The trip that changed me most was a slow week in Vietnam. (B) 2. I feel most relaxed when the train leaves the city behind. (A) 3. A place I know, where the nights are quiet, keeps calling me back. (B) 4. I have realised that good travel means leaving gaps in the plan. (C) 5. Unless I download the map, I always get lost. (A) 6. Could you tell me how early I should reach the airport? (C)

Answer key. Select to open.

Accept true lines. After you write, label each one A, B, or C.

Activity 14. Speak: sort the overbooked hotel

Your hotel gave your room away. You are at the desk. Use at least 1 A, 1 B, and 1 C.

Start: Hello, I have a problem with ...
Help: Even though I booked months ago, ... / The confirmation that I have says ... / Could you tell me when ...?

Hello, I have a problem with the booking that I made in March. (B) Even though I booked months ago, my room was given away. (A) Could you tell me when a similar room will be free? (C) Unless you find one tonight, I will need a full refund. (A)

Answer key. Select to open.

No fixed script. Good if you used one of each type and the desk could follow you. Record your try and play it back once.

Activity 15. Exit ticket: 3 lines for your next trip

Write 3 lines you can really use when you travel.

1. Unless we book early, prices will double. (A) 2. I will rebook the riad where the courtyard was silent. (B) 3. I wonder whether the coastal bus runs on Sundays. (C)

Answer key. Select to open.

Accept real plans. Check word order after tell me, and whose for owning. Keep these 3 lines for next time.
