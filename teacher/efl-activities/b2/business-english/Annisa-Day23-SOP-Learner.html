12

Plan with story table Done

2 min

Choose one lens. Copy its row. Each move has a job.

My goal lens, choose one:

Story table, what each move means: Hook tells one moment. Journey tells 3 past steps. Proof gives 30 days, 90+, 20%. Goals tell degree plus why. Program names one lab or course.

Answer key

Both good. M is easiest. S adds duty to serve. Example M: M, audit moment, lab plus mentoring, 30 days 90+ 20%, master's design, systems lab. One named program must appear in your draft.
