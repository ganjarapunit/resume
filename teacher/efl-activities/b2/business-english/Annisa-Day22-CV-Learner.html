Today you will learn

How to match 3 CV bullets to a Training Assistant post and open a cover letter with fit plus proof. Same pilot numbers from Days 18 to 21. No new numbers.

You already know: past verbs from Day 16, numbers as proof from Days 18 to 21, polite opening from Day 19. Today we reuse them for a job.

Real examples we study: British Council CV (Maria Jones) and cover letter (Laura Mazzanti). Free to read, same frames as ours.

1. Ran. 2. Tracked. 3. Wrote. Post word plus her number each time.

a. Verb plus number. The real CV line proves it: involves working to tight deadlines to design, create and launch. Stuff is banned.

7

Cover opening Done

3 min

Copy all 3. Role, proof, glad.

Real cover letter (British Council, Laura Mazzanti): I am writing in response to the job advertisement for the position of sales manager. Subject: Application for sales manager position. See the full letter.

Model: Dear Hiring Manager, I am writing to apply for the Training Assistant role. I ran short daily training for 30 staff and reported results of 83 vs 70 to managers. I would be glad to bring clear training support and reporting to your centre.

Answer key

Copy all 3 lines exactly. The real cover proves the frame: specific subject plus I am writing in response to. Check role, 83 vs 70, glad.

13

Write your bullets 7 min Done

Write alone

3 bullets plus 1 opening, about 100 words.

Words: 0. Aim: about 100.
Frames to copy: Ran... Tracked... Reported... Wrote... I am writing to apply for... I would be glad to...
Answer key, models for Bullets and Opening

Bullets: Ran short 20 minute daily training sessions for 30 staff in a 2 week pilot. Tracked scores in simple tables and reported 83 vs 70, a gap of 13 points. Wrote short updates and polite emails that managers answered in one week.

Opening: Dear Hiring Manager, I am writing to apply for the Training Assistant role. I ran short daily training for 30 staff and reported results of 83 vs 70 to managers. I would be glad to bring clear training support and reporting to your centre.
