Three clause types at work

B1 | Work English | Use Back and Next to move. 15 activities plus cover and finish.

A = adverbial: when, why, if B = attributive: describes a noun C = noun clause: after think, know, tell

Because the shop was busy, I stayed late. The rota that is by the till has the hours. I think that Friday will be busy. Three links, one workday.

How this deck works. Select to open.

One slide at a time. Answer on the slide, press Check, open the key, then press Next. Say each answer aloud once.

Activity 1. Your work week

Speak. 1 or 2 sentences each.

1. What was busy this week?
2. Who helped you at work?
3. What do you want to finish next week?

I was busy because we had a stock check. Lan helped me. She is the colleague who knows the system. I want to finish the report that is late.

Answer key. Select to open.

No fixed answers. Any true reply is good. Aim for one because, who, or that line.

Activity 2. Which sounds better at work?

Choose a or b.

1. a) Meeting at 10. Be there. b) When the meeting starts at 10, please be there. c) When the meeting starts at 10, but please be there.

2. a) I need the file. You sent yesterday. b) I need the file that you sent yesterday. c) I need the file you sent yesterday.

3. a) Where is the key? Tell me. b) Can you tell me where the key is? c) Can you tell me what the key is?

Answer key. Select to open.
  1. b, when clause gives time.
  2. b, that points to the right file.
  3. b, polite noun clause.

Activity 3. Sort: A, B, or C?

1. I stayed late because the client called.

2. She is the manager who hired me.

3. I know that the delivery is late.

4. If the printer works, print two copies.

5. The room where we meet is cold.

6. Do you know when the bus leaves?

7. Although it rained, we finished the stock check.

8. The form that you need is on my desk.

9. Can you tell me what time the shop opens?

Answer key. Select to open.

A: 1, 4, 7. B: 2, 5, 8. C: 3, 6, 9.

Activity 5. Adverbial: your true lines A

Say, then type. Short lines are best.

1. I hurry when ...
2. I call my manager if ...
3. I stayed late last week because ...
4. Although I was tired, I ...

I hurry when the phone rings at closing time. I call my manager if the till does not work. I stayed late because we had new stock. Although I was tired, I helped a new colleague.

Answer key. Select to open.

Accept true lines. Check each starts with the right linker and has a subject plus verb after it.

Activity 9. think, know, said C

1. I think ___ we need more staff on Saturdays.

2. She said ___ the new rota starts Monday.

3. I do not know ___ to do about the broken till.

4. He asked ___ I can work late on Friday.

5. I am not sure ___ the client paid or not.

1 that. 2 that. 3 what. 4 whether or if. 5 whether. Note: we often drop that in speech for 1 and 2.

Activity 11. Fix the chat: choose the right line

1. Although it was busy, but we helped all clients.

2. The driver which brought the boxes is waiting.

3. Do you know where is the new rota?

4. The room where we eat it is small.

5. She said me that she will be late.

6. If you will see Lan, tell her I called.

Answer key. Select to open.

All first options are right. 1: no but with although. 2: who for people. 3: statement order. 4: drop extra it. 5: told me, not said me. 6: if plus present.

Activity 12. Meetings: choose the natural reply

1. Boss: The file is late.

2. Client: Where is my order?

3. You need help.

4. Plan change.

Answer key. Select to open.

All first options. They are fuller, politer, and use the clause correctly.

Activity 14. Speak: fix a late delivery

A delivery is 2 days late. You call the supplier. Use at least 1 A, 1 B, and 1 C.

Start: Hello, I am calling about ...
Help: Because the boxes did not come, ... / The order that we need is ... / Can you tell me when ...?

Hello, I am calling about the order that we placed on Monday. (B) Because the boxes did not come, our shelves are empty. (A) Can you tell me when the van will arrive? (C) If it comes today, we can still serve customers. (A)

Answer key. Select to open.

No fixed script. Good if you used one of each type and the supplier could follow you. Record your try and play it back once.
