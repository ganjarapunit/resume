How to use this page

Zero Conditional - Office Rules and Policies

Fill the gaps

Complete these company rules

Project Post-Mortem - Third and Mixed

Speaking task

Last week's product launch failed. Use the problems below to write one Third Conditional sentence and one Mixed Conditional sentence for each.

Your analysis

Problem A: We didn't market the product on social media.

Model responses

Problem B (technical team): Third: "If our team had been experienced with cloud architecture, the servers wouldn't have crashed." Mixed: "If our team were experienced with cloud architecture, we wouldn't have lost three major clients last month."

Problem C (testing time): Third: "If we had allocated enough testing time, we would have found the bugs." Mixed: "If we had allocated more testing time last month, our reputation wouldn't be damaged today."
