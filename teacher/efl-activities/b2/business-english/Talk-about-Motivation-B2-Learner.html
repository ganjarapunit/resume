Talk about Motivation

A talk about incentives vs autonomy. How rewards can fail for creative work.

Tap Next. Read first. Then listen. Answer as you go.

How to use

  1. Read Activities 1 to 3 first.
  2. On Activities 4 to 9, use the Listen bar under the slide. Play twice.
  3. Choose or type. Tap Check. Then open Answer key.
  4. Speak aloud for Activities 13 to 15.

Our plan

Before: predict, words. While: gist, detail. After: use, speak.

One task at a time. Short steps. No rush.

Activity 1, Warmer

How are you today?

Choose one, then say it aloud: great, OK, tired, stressed, unmotivated.

Finish: Today I feel ___ because ___.

Answer key (try first)

Any true sentence. Example: Today I feel OK because I slept well.

Activity 2, Predict

What will we hear?

Our title: Talk about Motivation. Tick what you expect.

Choose the three true ideas. Check one at a time.

Answer key (try first)

how money motivates workers, why rewards sometimes fail, the future of work.

Activity 3, Words

Match the words

1 reward, 2 incentive, 3 automation, 4 creative, 5 problem-solving, 6 intrinsic motivation, 7 autonomy, 8 flexible working

Answer key (try first)

1 a. 2 e? Wait - the key says: 1f, 2a, 3h, 4b, 5c, 6d, 7g, 8e. But in the user text: 1 reward (f), 2 incentive (a), 3 automation (h), 4 creative (b), 5 problem-solving (c), 6 intrinsic motivation (d), 7 autonomy (g), 8 flexible working (e).

Activity 4, Gist

What is the talk about?

Answer key (try first)

Why incentives don't always work.

Activity 5, Rewards and simple tasks

What happened in the first experiment?

Answer key (try first)

The reward group took 3.5 minutes longer. The incentive made them slower.

Activity 6, Rewards and complex tasks

What does bigger reward do?

Answer key (try first)

The bigger the reward, the worse people perform. The reward made them focus so hard on the result that they couldn't think creatively.

Activity 7, Why incentives fail

Complete

Answer key (try first)

1 automated. 2 result. 3 don't work.

Activity 8, What works

What does work for motivation?

Answer key (try first)

Freedom to choose what, when and how you work.

Activity 9, Flexible working

Choose 3 you hear

Check one at a time.

Answer key (try first)

a, b, c.

Activity 10, Use the words

Choose the verb

Answer key (try first)

1 offer. 2 automate. 3 focus.

Activity 11, Opinion

Management styles

Say your opinion and explain briefly.

Answer key (try first)

b is better. Model: I think freedom is better because it lets me be more creative.

Activity 12, Vocabulary

Collocations

Answer key (try first)

1 a reward. 2 kids. 3 money.

Activity 13, Role play

Discussion on Motivation

Speak 6 lines:
Say: your view, reason, example, another view, suggestion, thank you.
Start: "I think the most important thing is..."

Answer key, model (speak first)

I think the most important thing is freedom to choose how we work. Some people like bonuses but I've seen creativity suffer. I'd suggest giving people time for their own projects. That way people are more motivated. Thank you for listening.

Activity 14, Reflection

What motivates you?

Say 3 sentences:
1 What kind of incentive motivates me most? ___
2 What kind of autonomy do I want? ___
3 One change I will make is ___

Answer key, model (speak first)

I am motivated by freedom and meaningful work. I want to choose when and where I work. I will try to organize my day so I have creative time in the morning.

Activity 15, Takeaway

Well done!

Answer key (try first)

Any personal answer. Example: Next time I will mention the importance of autonomy. Stars depend on your rating.

Activity 16, Complex Sentences

Join them

Join the two sentences using the word in brackets.

Answer key (try first)

1 Rewards work for simple tasks but they don't work for creative tasks. 2 Incentives made people slower because they were offered money. 3 People need freedom to choose when they work so that they get better results.

Activity 17, Complex Sentences

Build them

Finish the sentences using your own ideas.

Answer key (try first)

Possible answers: 1 ...so we'll need people to do more creative work. 2 ...when they have freedom to choose their tasks. 3 ...they don't work well for creative work. Accept any grammatically correct complex sentences.

Activity 18, Complex Sentences

Apply to real life

Write or say 3 complex sentences about motivation at work. Use: although, because, even though, so that, as long as.

Answer key (try first)

Model: Although many companies offer bonuses, I think freedom is more motivating because it allows me to be creative. People work better when they have autonomy, so companies should give them more flexibility. Even though money is important, intrinsic motivation is stronger for complex tasks.
