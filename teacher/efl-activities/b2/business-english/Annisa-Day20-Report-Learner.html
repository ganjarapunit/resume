Read our model Done

3 min

Read one time. Then answer.

TITLEStaff training pilot, March update.

SUMMARYOverall, short daily sessions did clearly better than long late sessions in our two week pilot with 60 staff.

FINDINGSGroup A did short 20 minute sessions each day and scored 83 on the final skills test. Group B did long 90 minute late sessions and scored 70. This shows that Group A scored 13 points higher than Group B, compared to the same test. This suggests that short regular training helps staff learn and stay fresh.

NEXTWe plan to keep short daily sessions for the next team and check results again in one month.

About 140 words. We use this report all lesson. No new reading.

Write alone

120 to 150 words. Use Title, Summary, Findings, Next steps.

Words: 0. Aim: 120 to 150.
Frames to copy: Overall... compared to... higher than... This shows that... This suggests that... We plan to...
Answer key, models for Ending M and Ending E

M model, about 140 words: Title: Staff training pilot, March update. Summary: Overall, short daily sessions did clearly better than long late sessions in our two week pilot with 60 staff. Findings: Group A did short 20 minute sessions each day and scored 83 on the final skills test. Group B did long 90 minute late sessions and scored 70. This shows that Group A scored 13 points higher than Group B, compared to the same test. This suggests that short regular training helps staff learn and stay fresh. Next steps: We plan to keep short daily sessions for the next team and check results again in one month.

E model, about 140 words: Title: Staff training pilot, March update. Summary: Overall, long late sessions did clearly worse in our two week pilot with 60 staff. Findings: Group A scored 83 on the final skills test, compared to 70 for Group B. This shows a gap of 13 points higher than Group B on the same test. This suggests that long late training may cause tiredness, which is linked to lower scores. Next steps: We plan to move late sessions to short morning slots and check scores again in one month.
