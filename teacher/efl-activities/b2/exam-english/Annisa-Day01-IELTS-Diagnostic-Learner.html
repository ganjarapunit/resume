Day 1, Let us see your level

A calm first step. We learn how IELTS is scored, we write a little together, then we set your 3 goals. No stress, we go slow.

  • Type your answers, then press Check my answers.

  • Press Mark complete to fill the progress bar.
  • Finish all 10 to complete your practice.
Your 4 scores today: Task (did you answer all parts), Organization (is it clear), Vocabulary (are words natural), Grammar (are sentences correct). We use 1 to 5. Small steps are good.

Chart Check

Task 1, overview DONE

Find the good overview. A good overview has 2 big trends, no numbers.

Look at the chart, then read the two texts.

Japan7 hours5 hours

Text A: "The chart shows homework hours in Japan, Germany, Brazil. In 2020 Brazil was highest at 9 hours. Japan fell to 5. Big change for Brazil."

Text B: "Overall, students in Brazil spent more time on homework than in Japan and Germany, and this gap grew between 2010 and 2020."

Which text has a clear overview?
Why is that text better?
Tip to remember: A good overview has 2 big trends. No numbers in the overview, numbers go in the next paragraphs.

Essay Check

Task 2, thesis DONE

Find the clear thesis. A clear thesis gives opinion plus plan.

Question: Some people think online learning is better than classroom learning. Do you agree or disagree?

Thesis 1: "I partly agree because online learning has benefits and also problems."

Thesis 2: "I disagree. Online learning is flexible, but it weakens student interaction, so classrooms remain better overall."

Which thesis is clearer?
Why?

Say it aloud

The most marked change was in Brazil, where weekly homework rose from 6 to 9 hours. By contrast, homework time in Japan fell from 7 to 5 hours, while Germany remained stable at 6 hours.

What is the correct PEEL order?
PEEL is for all writing, not only IELTS. You will use it again from Day 10.
One clear advantage of online learning is flexibility. For example, I can watch a recorded lesson again when I miss a point, which is hard in a busy classroom. This repeated access helps me understand difficult ideas without slowing other students. Even so, this flexibility alone does not make online learning better overall, because interaction with teachers still matters.
