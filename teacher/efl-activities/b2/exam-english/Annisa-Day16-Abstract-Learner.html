Read our model Done

3 min

Read one time. Then answer.

BMany students spend long late hours on homework and lose focus. Their habits become weak, and learning feels slow. Both Lee (2023) and Smith (2024) suggest short balanced tasks work best.

AThis study aims to test if short daily homework builds stronger habits.

MSixty students joined the study for two weeks in March. Group A did short 20 minute tasks each day. Group B did long 90 minute late tasks. All students took the same habit test at the end.

RResults show that Group A scored 18 percent higher than Group B.

CIn conclusion, short daily homework may build stronger habits and protect focus.

104 words. Same Lee plus Smith idea from Day 15. We use this text all lesson. No new reading.

What helped habits?

Answer key

a. Short daily tasks helped. Group A scored 18 percent higher. Same shared idea as Day 15.

Name the 5 parts Done

3 min

Write one letter: B, A, M, R, C.

1. This study aims to test if... is
2. Sixty students joined... is
3. Many students spend long late hours... is
4. Results show that Group A scored... is
5. In conclusion, short homework may build... is
Remember: B A M R C. Problem, goal, what you did, what you found, what it means.
Answer key
  1. A, aim
  2. M, method
  3. B, background
  4. R, results
  5. C, conclusion

6

Careful results Done

2 min

Good research is careful. No big claims.

Which sentence is best? Same careful rule as Day 13.

Which ending is careful?

Words to use: show, suggest, may, compared to. Avoid: prove, perfect, always.
Answer key

First: b. Second: a. Give numbers. Stay careful with may and show.

7

Put it in order Done

3 min

Write 1 to 5. 1 is first.

A. Results show Group A scored higher.
B. Many students lose focus after long hours.
C. In conclusion, short homework may help.
D. This study aims to test short homework.
E. Sixty students joined for two weeks.
Answer key

B 1, D 2, E 3, A 4, C 5. Same as B A M R C.

8

Make it short Done

3 min

Say the same. Use fewer words.

1. Wordy: Due to the fact that students were very tired, they could not focus in an effective way.

2. Wordy: The results of the study were of a kind that showed an improvement in the scores.

3. Wordy: In conclusion, it can be said that it is possible that short tasks might be helpful.

Tip: Cut due to the fact that, of a kind that, it can be said that. Keep numbers.
Answer key
  • 1. Because students were tired, they could not focus well.
  • 2. Results showed higher scores.
  • 3. In conclusion, short tasks may help.

Your words can differ. Short and clear is good. Keep numbers if the sentence has one.

Which tense? Done

2 min

Choose the correct verb.

1. Background: Many students ___ tired after long hours.

2. Method: Sixty students ___ the study in March.

3. Conclusion: Short homework ___ build habits.

Answer key

1. feel. 2. joined. 3. may. Background present, method past, conclusion present with may.

Build one together Done

5 min

First new numbers today. Same B A M R C order. Do with your teacher.

Topic: sleep and vocabulary. Data: 40 students, 3 weeks. Group A slept 8 hours. Group B slept 6 hours. Test: 15 word test. A remembered 12. B remembered 9.

1. Background: Many studentsbefore tests.
2. Aim: This studytest if sleep helps memory.
3. Method: Forty studentsthe study for three weeks.
4. ResultsGroup A remembered more words.
Say it first: Tell your teacher each sentence. Then type. Slow is good.
Answer key

Model lines: Many students forget words before tests. This study aims to test if sleep helps memory. Forty students joined the study for three weeks. Results show that Group A remembered more words, 12 compared to 9. In conclusion, enough sleep may help memory. Your words can differ slightly.

2 min

Group B: long tasks, 90 minutes late at night.
Test: same habit test at the end.

Group A: slept 8 hours each night.
Group B: slept 6 hours each night.
Test: same 15 word test at the end.
Result: Group A remembered 12 words, Group B remembered 9 words.

Step 1: My topic is:

Answer key

Both topics are good. A is easiest because the numbers match the Act 2 model. Step 2 answers: A gives 60 students, 2 weeks, 18 percent higher. B gives 40 students, 3 weeks, 12 vs 9 words. Keep these same 3 numbers in your Activity 13 draft.
